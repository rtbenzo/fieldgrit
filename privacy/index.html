Privacy

NetCore LLC runs FieldGrit. This page says what the app handles. Updated November 22, 2026.

The account

Sign-in uses Apple, Google, or Microsoft. FieldGrit receives the name and email those services provide so the person can join one company. The first person is the admin. Later people join by invite.

The job

The company stores the job name, the customer, the address, notes, the checklist, the schedule, the status, and the costs. Photos, video, and voice notes are stored with the job. A photo can include the place it was taken. A customer link shows that job, including new shots, to the person who has the link.

Face ID

Face ID stays on the iPhone. It unlocks FieldGrit. FieldGrit does not receive a face image and does not send one anywhere.

QuickBooks

If the company connects QuickBooks, FieldGrit sends the job they choose to that QuickBooks account. Nothing is sent until someone uses that button.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no tracking across other companies’ apps.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • No use of job photos to train a public model.

How long it stays

The company record stays while the subscription is active. Email richbenson2@icloud.com to ask for a company record to be deleted. Sign-in with Apple can also be revoked in the Apple ID settings.

Children

FieldGrit is a business tool. It is not directed at children under 13.
