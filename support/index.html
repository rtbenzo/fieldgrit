Sign in

The first person to sign in with Apple, Google, or Microsoft becomes the admin. Everyone else needs an invite to that company. Users do not pick their own role.

The invoice

The name on the PDF is the company name saved in FieldGrit. FieldGrit does not print its own name as the business on the bill.

The lock

Face ID, with the device passcode as the fallback, opens the lock screen. Turning the lock on or off asks for Face ID.

A subscription

If FieldGrit is purchased as a subscription, Apple bills it. Change or cancel it in Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions on the iPhone.
