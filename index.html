The crew, the day, and the bill.

FieldGrit is the iPhone app for the company that does the work.

One company. The first person is the admin. Everyone else comes in by invite, as a manager, crew, or field tech. Put the job on the calendar, color it by status, shoot the work, and send the invoice as a PDF. The company name on the bill is the company that bought FieldGrit.

On the phone. Jobs, the calendar, costs, and the invoice sit on the bottom bar. Face ID can lock the app.

For the trades. HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping, cleaning, and the rest of the crew trades.

The books. Send a job to QuickBooks when the books need it. FieldGrit does not collect the customer’s card payment.
